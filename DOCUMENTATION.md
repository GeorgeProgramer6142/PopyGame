##### PopyGame
*by TreeGameStudio*
Description:
**Did you anytime wanted to catch a parrot? Or the gorilla? Or the hare? You can do it in this game.**
#### How to play:
**movement:**
W or arrow up: go up
A or arrow right: go right
S or arrow left: go left
D or arrow down: go down
L or Z: ability
ability + 1: fast running ability
M or X: interaction
